1. The Problem We're Positioning Against This is the core insight the whole offer is built on — lead with it in every pitch, because it's what makes “done-for-you” worth paying more for. Most real estate businesses assemble their marketing from scattered sources — a landing page from one freelancer, videos from another, ads run by someone else with no shared strategy. Nothing is built to work together, so even when ads run, the funnel leaks at every step and the business loses money without knowing why. 
2. Core Positioning & Messaging “We build and run the entire lead generation funnel for real estate businesses — landing pages, ad creatives, video, Meta ads, and automated lead qualification — so you're not stitching together freelancers and losing money on a broken funnel. One system, fully managed, built to convert.” Tagline options: 
◆ Your Entire Lead Funnel. Built, Managed, and Optimized — Done For You. 
◆ Stop Patching Together Freelancers. Get One Funnel That Actually Converts. 
◆ From Ad to Qualified Lead — We Own the Whole Journey. One-line elevator pitch: “We're the team real estate businesses hire when they're tired of losing money on marketing that doesn't talk to itself.” WHY THIS WORKS — the differentiator isn't “we run ads” — every competitor says that. It's “we own the whole system so nothing falls through the cracks,” which directly answers why their past efforts failed. 
3. Who This Is For (Ideal Client Profile) Keep targeting broad for now, but this is the mental filter for qualifying conversations — good-fit prospects usually show most of these signs: 
◆ Already spending money on ads, portals, or freelancers — but not tracking cost per qualified lead 
◆ Has an active property/project to sell or a consistent supply of listings 
◆ Frustrated by “leads” that turn out to be tyre-kickers or wrong-fit buyers 
◆ Has a sales team or personally follows up — needs leads that are actually ready to talk 
◆ Values a working system over the cheapest possible price Weaker-fit signals (proceed carefully): 
◆ Wants leads with zero ad budget 
◆ Has no active inventory or project to promote 
◆ Looking for the lowest price rather than a working funnel

11. Objection Handling “Why should I pay a setup fee upfront?” The setup fee covers real production work — the landing page, video, and automation are custom-built for your business, not templated. It also means we're both committed before spending a rupee on ads. “How is this different from just running ads?” Running ads without a proper funnel is like pouring water into a leaking bucket. We build the landing page, qualification, and follow-up together with the ads — so traffic actually converts instead of leaking out. “What if the leads still aren't good?” That's what the guarantee is for — we optimize the funnel until it hits the agreed qualified lead volume, or leads that don't pass the qualification filter don't count toward the invoice, depending on which guarantee is agreed. “Can I just get ads without the landing page and video?” We could, but ads alone rarely convert well without a matching landing page and qualification step — that mismatch is usually why past campaigns underperformed.
