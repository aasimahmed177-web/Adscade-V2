Landing Page Script — "The Complete Buyer Funnel System" Targeting: Real Estate Business Owners, Brokers & Agents (B2B) For Google Demand Gen Campaigns (Policy-Compliant Draft) ⚠ Before You Build — Compliance Checklist 
● [ ] Page served over HTTPS 
● [ ] Fully mobile-responsive 
● [ ] Headline on page matches the ad headline used in Demand Gen creative 
● [ ] Privacy Policy linked in footer and near the form 
● [ ] Business name, contact email/phone visible in footer 
● [ ] No pop-ups blocking the offer on load 
● [ ] No guaranteed income/results claims without a clear qualifier (see Section 6) 
● [ ] Form has a consent checkbox for data usage 
● [ ] Avoid trigger words: guaranteed, best, no risk, miracle, instant, 100%, secret 
● [ ] If using the "20 leads in 30 days" guarantee, state the actual conditions of that guarantee somewhere on the page (don't leave it as an unqualified promise) 
1. Hero Section Headline (must match ad headline exactly): Stop Losing Money on Scattered Real Estate Marketing Sub-headline: We build and manage your entire buyer lead funnel — landing page, video, Meta ads, and automated qualification — so every lead that reaches you is ready to talk. Primary CTA button: Get My Free Funnel Audit Compliance note: "Free Funnel Audit" is fine as a CTA as long as it's genuinely free with no hidden conditions — if there's a catch, disclose it near the button. 
2. Trust Bar ✔ Done-for-you setup | ✔ WhatsApp-qualified leads | ✔ Weekly performance reporting Compliance note: only include claims you can back up if asked — avoid unverifiable stats like "500+ clients served" unless true and documentable. 
3. Problem/Agitation Section Heading: Sound Familiar? Body copy: Landing page from one freelancer. Videos from another. Ads run by someone with no shared strategy. Nothing built to work together — so even when your ads run, the funnel leaks, and you lose money without knowing exactly why. Compliance note: this is a general industry observation, not a claim about a specific competitor — avoid naming or clearly implying any competitor by name, which Google's policy on disparaging content restricts. 
4. The Offer Heading: One System. Built, Managed, and Optimized For You. Sub-heading: The Complete Buyer Funnel System What's included (bullets): 
● Funnel strategy built around your specific property/project 
● High-converting landing page with built-in buyer qualification 
● Video ad creatives made specifically for your funnel 
● Meta ads management — built, launched, and optimized 
● Automated WhatsApp/SMS follow-up sequence 
● Weekly reporting — cost per qualified lead, funnel drop-off, conversion rate Compliance note: describing your own deliverables factually is fine — avoid phrases like "the only agency that..." or "#1 real estate marketing service" unless independently verifiable. 
5. Old Way vs. Our Way (Comparison Block) Scattered Approach Complete Funnel System Landing page, video, ads from different freelancers One team builds and owns the entire funnel No qualification — every lead treated the same Built-in qualifying questions filter serious buyers No follow-up after the click Automated messaging sequence nurtures every lead Unclear where the funnel is leaking Weekly reporting shows drop-off at every stage Compliance note: comparison tables are allowed as long as they compare "old approach vs. new approach" generically rather than naming or clearly identifying a specific competitor. 
6. Guarantee Section Heading: Our Guarantee Body copy (choose the version matching what you'll actually honor): We build your complete funnel live within 3 weeks. If it doesn't generate qualified leads within 30 days of launch, we continue optimizing at no additional cost until it does. (Terms: "qualified lead" is defined as a submission that meets the agreed budget, location, and timeline criteria set during onboarding.) Compliance note: this is the single most important compliance element on the page. An unqualified guarantee ("guaranteed leads or your money back," full stop) reads as a misrepresentation risk. Defining "qualified lead" and the conditions turns it from a vague promise into a clear, honest commercial term — which is what Google's policy team looks for when a guarantee is challenged. 
7. Lead Capture Form Form heading: Get Your Free Funnel Audit Fields: 
1. Full Name 
2. Business Name 
3. Phone Number (with WhatsApp opt-in checkbox) 
4. City/Region of Operation 
5. Current Marketing Setup (dropdown: Running ads myself / Using freelancers / Using an agency / Not running ads yet) Required consent checkbox (not pre-checked): ☐ I agree to be contacted via phone/WhatsApp regarding this inquiry, and I have read the [Privacy Policy]. CTA button: Request My Free Audit 
8. Social Proof Section Heading: Results Speak for Themselves Guidance (do not fabricate): 
● Use real client results only, with permission to publish 
● If specific numbers are shown (e.g., "reduced cost per lead by X%"), keep documentation on file in case of a policy review 
● If no case studies exist yet, replace with a neutral credibility statement: "We work exclusively with real estate businesses — every funnel we build is tailored to how property buyers actually search and decide." Compliance note: fabricated testimonials or unverifiable performance numbers are treated as a serious misrepresentation violation, not just a disapproval — this can risk the whole ad account. 
9. FAQ Section Heading: Common Questions 
● How much does this cost? State the setup fee + retainer structure clearly, or say "Pricing is discussed on the audit call" — don't leave pricing fully hidden if the ad implies a specific offer. 
● What counts as a "qualified lead"? Repeat the definition from the guarantee section for consistency. 
● Do I need to already be running ads? Answer honestly — this also naturally qualifies the lead before the call. 
10. Footer (Required Elements) 
● Business name: Nirmal Digital Media 
● Contact email and phone number 
● City/region (Lucknow, Uttar Pradesh, or full address if available) 
● Link to Privacy Policy 
● Link to Terms of Use (recommended) 
● Disclaimer line: Results vary based on market conditions, ad budget, and property type. Past performance with other clients does not guarantee identical results. Compliance note: this disclaimer directly protects the guarantee claim in Section 6 — pairing a strong guarantee with a fair disclaimer is standard practice and reduces policy risk. Words/Phrases to Avoid Site-Wide Avoid Use Instead "Guaranteed leads, no questions asked" "Qualified leads, guaranteed — terms apply" (link to definition) "#1 real estate marketing agency" "Trusted by real estate businesses in [region]" (only if true) "Instant results" "Funnel live within 3 weeks" "Every other agency fails you" "Most funnels fail because of scattered execution" (no direct competitor naming) "100% qualified leads" "Leads filtered against your specific criteria" Notes for the Client 
1. This page is B2B — it's speaking to a real estate business owner evaluating a vendor, not a home buyer. Tone should be consultative and credibility-driven, not salesy. 
2. The guarantee section is the highest-risk area for policy disapproval — always pair any guarantee with a plain-language definition of what qualifies. 
3. Since this runs on Demand Gen (YouTube, Discover, Gmail placements), make sure the video ad creative's claims match exactly what's written on this page — mismatches between ad and landing page are a common cause of disapproval. 
4. Keep documentation (screenshots, client agreements) for any specific result claims used in testimonials, in case Google's policy team requests verification.
