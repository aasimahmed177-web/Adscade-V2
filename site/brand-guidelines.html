Adscade Brand Guidelines

Version 1.0 · 30 July 2026. Rendered in the actual brand styles. Full written spec: docs/ADSCADE_BRAND_GUIDELINES.md

The name is Adscade. One capital A. Never AdScade, AdsCade, Adscape or adScade — in copy, code, filenames, ad accounts or invoices.

Positioning

Adscade builds the system between paid attention and qualified pipeline.

Tagline: From paid attention to qualified pipeline.
Service descriptor: Real Estate Acquisition Systems

The tagline is the master-brand line. It does not replace a campaign landing page headline — ad-to-page match beats brand consistency on paid traffic, every time.

Rules

  • Gold for emphasis — buttons, eyebrows, single words, data. Never for paragraphs.
  • Green panels are functional (you interact). Brown-black panels are editorial (you read).
  • Red is an error colour, not a brand colour. Never decorative.
  • No royal blue anywhere. If blue appears it is a host-theme leak.

Typography

Two families. Never three. Instrument Serif for display, Inter Tight for everything else.

Display italic once

One italicised phrase per headline. Never two.

Buttons

Check Fit & Pick a Time Book a Fit Call

No gradients, glow, bounce or pulsing. Hover is a 2px lift. Radius scale: 0 · 2 · 4 · 10 · 14 · 50% · 999px. Nothing else.

Pipeline diagram language

Paid attention→Message→Landing experience→ Qualification→Follow-up→Sales pipeline→ Revenue feedback

The house motif. Reuse across site, decks, proposals, social and reports — it is the most recognisable thing the brand owns.

Voice

British/Indian English. Short, complete sentences. Explain the commercial consequence. Distinguish enquiries → qualified enquiries → booked calls → site visits → revenue; collapsing those is how vendors mislead, and this buyer notices.

Write this

  • "You are not short of enquiries. You are losing them in transit."
  • "Every Monday, know where every enquiry stands."
  • "We don't promise a number — that depends on your inventory, your price and your market."

Never this

  • "10X your real estate sales with our revolutionary AI-powered funnel."
  • "We guarantee 50 qualified leads in 30 days."
  • "India's #1 real estate marketing agency."

Brand architecture

Master brand Adscade. Descriptors sit beneath it, never beside it: Adscade Real Estate Acquisition System · Adscade YouTube Acquisition System · Adscade Lead Qualification System. One logo, palette, type system, voice and UI kit across all of them.

Enforcement

Most of this is machine-checked: node tools/score.mjs verifies palette, families, contrast, tap targets, radius discipline and banned phrases; node tools/e2e.mjs verifies CTA consistency, no decorative red, 16px reading floor and no horizontal overflow.
