Adscade exists because advertising is almost never the part that is broken. The campaign runs, the clicks arrive, and the loss happens somewhere between the click and the sales conversation — in the page, the qualification, or the follow-up. Nobody owning one piece can see that.

So I run the pieces together. I oversee the offer and messaging, the campaigns, the landing page and qualification, the tracking, and the weekly reporting. For a developer that matters more than for most businesses: enquiry volume is easy, but a site visit from someone who can actually afford the flat is the only number that moves inventory.

"Most developers don't have a demand problem. They have a leak — and nobody has ever shown them where it is."
