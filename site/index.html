Adscade exists because ads, landing pages, qualification, follow-up and sales reporting are almost always handled as separate activities. Each one looks fine on its own. The loss happens in the gaps between them, and nobody owning a single piece can see it.

I approach acquisition as one connected commercial system, and I personally oversee the strategy and the system design. The objective is qualified buyer pipeline and visibility from enquiry through to site visit — not enquiry volume, which is the easiest number in this industry to produce and the least useful one to receive.
