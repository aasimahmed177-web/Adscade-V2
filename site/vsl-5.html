The expertise is already there

Good advice deserves a bigger audience.

Every week, your team explains payment plans, compares areas and answers the questions that hold buyers back. That knowledge is worth sharing beyond a phone call or a meeting.

But someone still has to choose the topics, write the scripts, organise the shoot and get the videos out. When that work sits beside everyone’s day job, posting slips.

Adscade takes responsibility for that process—so your team can share what it knows without managing a content operation.

What your videos could cover

Answer the questions buyers actually ask.

Build the month around your market, your projects and your team’s point of view. For example:

  1. 01

    “What will this property really cost me?”

    Explain the costs a buyer should account for beyond the advertised price.

  2. 02

    “Which area is right for my family?”

    Talk through the practical trade-offs your agents see on the ground.

  3. 03

    “How do I compare these two projects?”

    Give useful context on the payment plan, location and intended buyer.

  4. 04

    “What should I ask before I book a viewing?”

    Help buyers arrive better prepared—and show how your team thinks.
