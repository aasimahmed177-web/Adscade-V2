Privacy Policy

This policy explains what Adscade collects when you use this site, why we collect it, and what you can ask us to do about it. It is written to be read, not to be survived.

Who we are

Adscade is a marketing services business operating from Bengaluru, Karnataka, India. For any question about this policy or your data, write to admin@adscade.com.

What we collect

Only what you type into the enquiry form on this site — five things and a consent tick:

  • Your name
  • Your work email address
  • Your WhatsApp or phone number
  • How many residential units you are actively marketing
  • Your current monthly advertising budget range

Alongside those, we record the campaign parameters in the link you arrived on (such as utm_source), the page you landed on, the page that referred you, whether you are on a phone, tablet or computer, and the identifier your browser sends describing itself and your operating system (its “user-agent”), which we keep only to diagnose faults. We do not ask for your company name, your project name or your city.

We do not run advertising pixels, session recording, or third-party analytics on this page. We do not buy or append data about you from anywhere else.

Why we collect it

To contact you about the call you asked for, and so that call starts with your numbers rather than twenty minutes of background. Nothing you submit is assessed automatically — there is no scoring, and no answer you give decides whether you can book a time. Everyone who submits the form is offered the calendar, and whether we are a good fit is something we work out together on the call.

We contact you only about your enquiry. We do not add you to a newsletter, and we do not sell, rent or share your details with any other business.

Consent

The form will not submit unless you tick the consent box. That tick is your permission for us to contact you by phone, WhatsApp or email about this enquiry. You can withdraw it at any time by replying STOP to any message from us, or by emailing admin@adscade.com. Withdrawing consent does not affect anything we did while it was in place.

How long we keep it

Enquiries that become clients are retained for the duration of the engagement and for three years afterwards, for tax and contractual records. Enquiries that do not become clients are deleted within twelve months. Ask us sooner and we will delete them sooner.

Where it is stored

Submissions are stored with our hosting and database provider and are accessible only to Adscade staff working on your enquiry. Access is limited to the people who need it.

Scheduling your call

After you submit the form, a Calendly booking calendar is loaded into the page so you can choose a time. Calendly is a third-party scheduling service with its own privacy policy; when the calendar loads, it may set cookies and receives your browser's technical information.

Your name, email address and phone number are passed to Calendly so you do not have to type them again. Your answers about inventory and advertising budget are not passed to Calendly. If you close the calendar without booking, no booking is created — but the details you submitted have already been saved to our records, and you can ask us to delete them at any time using the contact address below.

Your rights

Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection Act, 2023, you can ask us to:

  • Tell you what data of yours we hold
  • Correct anything that is wrong
  • Delete it
  • Nominate someone to exercise these rights on your behalf

Email admin@adscade.com and we will respond within thirty days. If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the Data Protection Board of India.

Cookies

Adscade sets no cookies of its own. The Calendly booking calendar, once it loads into the page after you submit the form, sets its own cookies under Calendly's privacy policy. Typefaces are loaded from Google Fonts, which receives the request as a normal web request; no identifier is set by us.

Changes

If this policy changes materially, the date below changes with it. Anyone who has an open enquiry with us at the time will be told directly.
